Backend compatibility

This frontend uses the Version 4.2 JSONP GET API. Keep your Apps Script deployment active and set access according to your school's authorization requirements.

Student records and attendance are stored in your Google Sheets database. Do not share the Web App URL with people who should not have access to the system.
